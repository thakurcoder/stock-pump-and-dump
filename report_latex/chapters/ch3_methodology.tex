\section{Research Design}
The research employs a rigorous design-and-evaluate engineering methodology. The platform is designed around two decoupled, high-performance engines:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{The PumpWatch Quantitative Surveillance Pipeline:} An automated pipeline that performs feature extraction, rolling parameter standardization, and risk classification across cash equities.
    \item \textbf{The Institutional Derivatives Execution Engine:} An options analytics module that extracts, normalizes, and analyzes live exchange order books to generate multi-leg strategy recommendations.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Mathematical & Quantitative Formulations}

\subsection{Standardized Rolling Volume Z-Score ($Z_V$)}
In raw trading data, volume exhibits strong non-stationarity and varies by orders of magnitude across tickers. To establish cross-ticker statistical comparability, SentinelMarket standardizes current volume against a 20-day historical window ($N=20$):

\begin{equation}
\mu_V = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} V_{t-i}
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
\sigma_V = \sqrt{\frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} (V_{t-i} - \mu_V)^2}
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
Z_V = \frac{V_t - \mu_V}{\sigma_V}
\end{equation}

Where $V_t$ represents the current trading volume, $\mu_V$ is the rolling mean volume, and $\sigma_V$ is the historical standard deviation. In standard Gaussian distributions, a $Z_V > 3.0$ represents an event exceeding three standard deviations ($p < 0.0013$), indicating extreme statistical rarity.

\subsection{Volume Ratio Metric ($R_V$)}
To protect against volatility distortions caused by outlier days in the rolling mean, SentinelMarket also measures the volume multiple against the 20-day rolling median:

\begin{equation}
R_V = \frac{V_t}{\text{Median}(V_{t-1}, V_{t-2}, \dots, V_{t-20})}
\end{equation}

A security exhibiting $R_V > 3.0$ and $Z_V > 2.5$ is flagged for elevated surveillance.

\subsection{Wilder's Relative Strength Index (RSI)}
Momentum acceleration is tracked using Wilder's 14-period RSI:

\begin{equation}
\text{RS} = \frac{\text{EMA}_{14}(\text{Upward Price Changes})}{\text{EMA}_{14}(\text{Downward Price Changes})}
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
\text{RSI} = 100 - \left( \frac{100}{1 + \text{RS}} \right)
\end{equation}

An $\text{RSI} > 70$ indicates an overbought regime, while $\text{RSI} > 80$ paired with anomalous volume signifies an aggressive momentum pump.

\subsection{Bollinger Band Upper Breach Metric}
The upper volatility envelope is defined via a 20-day Simple Moving Average (SMA) and price standard deviation ($\sigma_P$):

\begin{equation}
\text{UpperBand} = \text{SMA}_{20}(P) + 2 \cdot \sigma_P
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
\Delta_{\text{BB}} = \frac{P_t - \text{UpperBand}}{\text{UpperBand}}
\end{equation}

When $P_t > \text{UpperBand}$ ($\Delta_{\text{BB}} > 0$), the security is in an extreme statistical expansion phase.

\subsection{Composite Manipulation Risk Score Algorithm}
The composite risk score $S \in [0, 100]$ is computed as a weighted sum of normalized penalty factors:

\begin{equation}
S = \min\left(100, \, w_1 \cdot f(Z_V) + w_2 \cdot f(R_V) + w_3 \cdot f(\text{RSI}) + w_4 \cdot f(\Delta_{\text{BB}}) + P_{\text{thin}}\right)
\end{equation}

Where weights $w_1 = 30$, $w_2 = 25$, $w_3 = 25$, and $w_4 = 20$, and $P_{\text{thin}} = 15$ is a penalty applied if the stock's average daily turnover is less than 5 Crore INR (indicating thin liquidity). \section{Institutional Derivatives Strategy Engine}

\subsection{Intraday vs. Positional OI Dynamics}
The derivatives engine parses the open contracts across 31 strikes around the current spot price:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Intraday Battleground:} Identified by the maximum daily change in Open Interest ($\Delta \text{OI}_{\text{PE}}$ for Support, $\Delta \text{OI}_{\text{CE}}$ for Resistance).
    \item \textbf{Positional Expiry Walls:} Identified by the maximum cumulative Open Interest ($\text{OI}_{\text{PE}}$ for Support, $\text{OI}_{\text{CE}}$ for Resistance).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Trapped Writer Gamma Panic Override}
When the underlying spot index breaks through a heavy open interest strike, option writers who sold naked contracts face unlimited theoretical loss. When $\Delta \text{OI} < -15\%$ occurs on intraday unwinding:

\begin{equation}
\text{PanicCondition} = \left( \Delta \text{OI}_{\text{strike}} < -0.15 \cdot \text{OI}_{\text{strike}} \right) \land \left( |S_t - K| < 0.005 \cdot S_t \right)
\end{equation}

The engine triggers an immediate **Step A Panic Override**, instructing traders to enter high-gamma directional debit spreads rather than selling credit.

\subsection{Strike Spread Width Filter}
To prevent selling option pairs into an explosive trending breakout, SentinelMarket calculates the strike distance:

\begin{equation}
D_{\text{strikes}} = |K_{\text{MaxCE}} - K_{\text{MaxPE}}|
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
\text{Threshold} = 0.0165 \cdot S_t
\end{equation}

If $D_{\text{strikes}} > \text{Threshold}$, the market is identified as a **Wide Range / Sideways Market**, recommending a Short Strangle pair. If $D_{\text{strikes}} \le \text{Threshold}$, a high-velocity directional breakout is diagnosed.

\section{Evaluation Metrics}
The quantitative performance of SentinelMarket is evaluated on:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Mathematical Zero-Leakage:} Ensuring no future-period data is leaked into rolling historical windows.
    \item \textbf{API Response Latency:} End-to-end roundtrip execution time from request receipt to JSON serialization ($<1500\text{ ms}$).
    \item \textbf{Fault Resilience:} Graceful degradation when external exchange endpoints return HTTP 403, 429, or format changes.
\end{enumerate}
