\begin{center}
{\LARGE \textbf{Abstract}}\\[1.2cm]
\end{center}

\noindent \textbf{SentinelMarket} is an institutional-grade, real-time financial market surveillance platform and derivatives risk playbook engineered to protect retail investors from fraudulent capital market schemes and bridge institutional information asymmetry. The platform directly tackles the proliferation of illicit ``Pump-and-Dump'' (P\&D) market manipulation syndicates and circular trading cartels active across Indian equities listed on the National Stock Exchange (NSE) and Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE), while concurrently providing high-conviction quantitative options strategies based on institutional Open Interest (OI) positioning.\\[0.4cm]

\noindent The primary technical contribution is the \textbf{PumpWatch Quantitative Anomaly Engine}, which ingests real-time tick-level and daily OHLCV market feeds across benchmark and user-configured equities without external database dependencies. The engine formulates a multi-factor mathematical scoring model incorporating standardized 20-day Volume Z-Scores ($Z_V$), rolling Volume Ratios against historical medians, Wilder's 14-period Relative Strength Index (RSI) momentum divergence, and Bollinger Band upper boundary breach metrics. These factors are dynamically synthesized into a composite \textbf{Manipulation Risk Score (0--100)} mapped to an intuitive traffic-light surveillance matrix: Clean Flow (0--20), Momentum (21--40), Volume Surge (41--60), and Critical Pump Alert (61--100).\\[0.4cm]

\noindent The secondary research contribution is an \textbf{Institutional F\&O Strategy Playbook and 31-Strike Option Chain Desk}. The engine evaluates live NIFTY 50 and BANK NIFTY derivatives books via official exchange feeds, isolating fast Intraday OI shifts from cumulative monthly Positional Expiry Walls. It features an automated \textbf{Trapped Writer Gamma Panic Override} (detecting short squeeze unwinding) and a \textbf{Spread Width Filter} ($\approx 1.65\%$ spot boundary) to mathematically discern high-probability directional trends from sideways chop, generating executable multi-leg strategies (Bull Call Spreads, Bear Put Spreads, or Credit Strangle Pairs).\\[0.4cm]

\noindent The system architecture is implemented as an ultra-responsive, decoupled client-server application consisting of a high-performance Python FastAPI asynchronous backend, browser impersonation telemetry clients, and a Bloomberg-inspired Dark Terminal frontend developed using Next.js 15 and Tailwind CSS v4. The platform includes full keyboard navigation shortcuts, live index ticker ribbons, interactive stock diagnostic modals, and persistent custom watchlists.\\[0.4cm]

\noindent Empirical software verification across 19 unit test cases and 8 automated integration test suites validates zero-latency mathematical scoring, synthetic fault tolerance, and deterministic execution. SentinelMarket demonstrates that complex, institutional-grade market surveillance and derivatives intelligence can be democratized in a transparent, reproducible, and accessible software artifact for retail participants.\\[0.8cm]

\noindent \textbf{Keywords:} Market Manipulation Surveillance, Pump-and-Dump Detection, Quantitative Finance, Volume Z-Score, Open Interest Analysis, Options Gamma Unwinding, High-Density Dark Terminal, FastAPI, Next.js, Algorithmic Trading.
