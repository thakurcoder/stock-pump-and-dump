\noindent This is to certify that the research project titled \textbf{``SentinelMarket: A Real-Time Equity Anomaly Surveillance and Institutional Options Risk Playbook for Indian Financial Markets''} has been carried out by \textbf{Aman Kumar Singh} during the course of his study in partial fulfillment of the requirements for the award of the degree of \textbf{Master of Computer Applications (MCA)} under the \textbf{University of Mumbai}, Mumbai, through Bharati Vidyapeeth's Institute of Management and Information Technology, Navi Mumbai, under our guidance and supervision.\\[0.6cm]

\noindent The research project encompasses the study, mathematical design, development, implementation, and experimental evaluation of an intelligent financial market surveillance platform integrating rolling Z-score volume analysis, relative strength momentum divergence, Bollinger Band breach detection, institutional Open Interest (OI) gamma unwinding, and an ultra-dense dark trading terminal.\\[2.5cm]
